# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Scope: niletherapy.org, Nile Therapy's public website (this repo). The staff portal in `../nile-therapy` is a separate product with its own context.

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Patients and their families, about equally.** Some visitors are adults booking for themselves (back, neck or knee pain, sports injuries, recovery after surgery). As many are relatives, often a son or daughter, booking home physiotherapy for an older parent or for someone recovering from surgery, a stroke or an ICU stay. Every page has to work for both: the person in pain and the person arranging care for someone else.
- **Physiotherapists who want to join the network.** The site has to serve them too. No recruitment page or form exists yet (open decision).
- **Partners and referrers.** Hospitals, clinics and sports clubs that refer patients. About has a `#partners` section, and "Partnerships" sits in the About menu.

Arabic is the main language (`/`), with English mirrored under `/en`. Most visitors are in Egypt and arrive on phones.

## Product Purpose

Nile Therapy is a home physiotherapy agency run by three founder physiotherapists. About 15 physiotherapists work for them remotely. The website exists to turn a search or a referral into a booked home visit. A booking reaches the founders either through the site's form, which creates a patient request in the portal, or through WhatsApp or a phone call.

The site is the #1 Google result for "Nile Therapy". Keeping that ranking is part of success, not a side concern.

## Positioning

These are the two claims to lead with:

1. **Specialist founders oversee the care.** The three co-founders are specialist physiotherapists: Dr. Mostafa Ismail and Dr. Ahmed Nader in neuromuscular physiotherapy, and Dr. Abdelrahman Sameh in cardiopulmonary, critical care and perioperative physiotherapy. They supervise treatment plans and follow up on them.
2. **A full assessment comes before any treatment.** The first visit is a home assessment of about 120 minutes (history, movement, strength, joint range, pain) that ends in a personal plan with goals, a suggested number of sessions and a home exercise program. Treatment comes after the root cause is found, not before.

The network across Egypt and the transparent cash prices are true facts and belong on the site, but they are not the lead claims.

## Operating Context

- Booking paths: the full booking page (`/hjz-almwaayd`, `/en/book`), the hero quick-book form (prefill handed over in sessionStorage, never in URLs), WhatsApp, and phone. On phones, a bottom bar offers Book, WhatsApp and Call.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Services and prices** (`src/content.mjs`): home assessment and first session, 800 EGP, about 120 min. Orthopedic and post-surgery sessions, 500 EGP. Neuro sessions, 500 EGP. Six specialty pages and articles in Arabic and English.
- **Stack**: static HTML/CSS/JS generated by `src/build.mjs` into `dist/` and deployed to GitHub Pages. There is no framework. The booking form posts to the Supabase Edge Function `submit-booking`, protected by Cloudflare Turnstile.
- **SEO constraints, binding**: every old URL keeps its exact address (no trailing slash). Every page needs a title, a description, a canonical URL pointing at `https://www.niletherapy.org`, and exactly one h1. The site keeps hreflang ar/en pairs, structured data, the sitemap and Google Analytics G-R0FRHE75S3. `scripts/check.mjs` enforces this.
- **Privacy**: personal data never goes in URLs, because GA would log it. Founders' phone numbers are not shown, by decision.
- **Medical copy** is general, conservative guidance. A founder reviews any change to it.
- **Coverage is not confirmed.** City pages are not built and the site must not list covered cities until the founders send the real list.
- **Open decisions**: a recruitment path for physiotherapists, and a dedicated partner or referrer path beyond the About section.

## Product Principles

1. **Booking is always one step away.** Every page leads to the booking form, WhatsApp or a call, and none of them asks the visitor to work for it.
2. **Earn trust with true things.** Real founders, real prices and a real process carry the weight. Unconfirmed numbers, filler reviews and invented coverage never appear.
3. **Speak to whoever is arranging care.** Copy and flows work as well for a relative booking for a parent as for the patient.
4. **Arabic first, English as an equal.** RTL is the primary layout, not an afterthought, and the English pages mirror the Arabic content.
5. **Never trade ranking for novelty.** URLs, page text, keywords and technical SEO are preserved through every redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Many patients and the relatives caring for them are older adults on phones. Text has to be readable, tap targets generous, and contact options plain to see.
- The layout has to be correct in RTL: bidirectional text (phone numbers, prices) is isolated, and the Egypt map SVG is forced LTR so its labels anchor correctly.
